# Sonny, this is your book

It is a book about our house. It is also a notebook, a mood board, and a
shopping list, and it lives on your phone. Nothing in it is finished, which is
the point.

## Reading it

Open the link I sent you. On the phone, swipe up and down like any book that
has been turned on its side. Tap the middle of a page and a small bar appears:
**Contents** jumps to any chapter, **Night** makes it kind to your eyes in bed,
and ⋯ holds the rest. On the laptop the pages turn with the arrow keys, a
swipe on the trackpad, or the arrows at the edges.

The first time, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** in Safari. Then it opens
like an app and works on the train, with no signal.

## Changing it

Tap **Edit**. It will ask who you are, once. After that every note and every
heart is signed by you.

- **Words.** Tap any title, caption or paragraph and type. Enter finishes a
  title. In an essay, select text for bold, italic, a link or a pull quote.
- **Pictures.** Tap an empty plate to choose a photo from your library, or drop
  one on it from a folder. Tap the crosshair to pick the part of the photo
  that must stay in view. Tap ⓘ for a caption and a credit. A photo that is
  too small for its place is shown framed, never stretched.
- **Notes.** **Note** adds a sticky note to the page. Drag it where it belongs,
  type on it, tick it when it is settled. Mine are green, yours are clay.
- **Hearts.** In any gallery, heart what you love. Tap the two little squares
  on up to three options and they line up side by side to compare.
- **Pages.** The strip along the bottom is the whole book. **Add** puts a new
  spread after the one you are on; **Template** changes its shape and keeps
  the words and pictures; drag the little pages to reorder.
- **Chapters.** Rename them, recolour them, reorder them. The contents page
  keeps up.
- **Undo** everything. The arrows in the toolbar, or ⌘Z on the laptop.

Everything saves by itself, on this phone, as you go. **Editions** keeps a
named copy of the whole book when you want a line in the sand.

## Adding our photos

Drop them on the pages as above, or hand them to me and I will put them in the
`content/photos` folder by chapter, in the order you want them to appear.

## Sharing it with me

For now the book lives separately on each phone, like two copies of the same
notebook. When you have made changes you want me to see: ⋯ → **Share / export**
→ **Share…** and send me the file (AirDrop, Messages, anything). I do the same
back. **Import** replaces the book on a phone with the file you were sent, and
keeps the old one as an edition, so nothing is ever lost.
