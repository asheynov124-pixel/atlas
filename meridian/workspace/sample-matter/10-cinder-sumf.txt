1. On December 15, 2025, Cinder gave Harbor written notice of a rate increase effective February 1, 2026. (02-cinder-correspondence.eml, l. 10)
2. Harbor did not object to the rate increase within thirty days. (02-cinder-correspondence.eml, l. 10)

4. Harbor waived any objection to the rate increase by continuing to accept shipments.

6. Cinder invoiced Harbor $184,250.00 at the increased rate, which remains unpaid. (Okafor Cert. ¶ 7)
7. Harbor gave no written notice of any defect within seven days of delivery. (02-cinder-correspondence.eml, l. 8)
8. Harbor continued to accept Cinder shipments through March 2026.
