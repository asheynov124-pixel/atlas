Our story

A college Friendsgiving

It all started with a Friendsgiving dinner during our college years, hosted by our incredible Maid of Honor, Allison. At the time, Sonny had no idea who Adi was. What caught her attention, though, was someone across the room confidently sharing his thoughts and keeping everyone engaged in conversation. Curious, she joined in, and what started as a lively conversation turned into the first chapter of our story.

Seven years

Neither of us could have imagined that one lively conversation would turn into seven unforgettable years together. Through every milestone, adventure, challenge, and celebration, we built a relationship rooted in laughter, unwavering support, and a friendship that only grew stronger with time.

The girlfriend forever

After seven wonderful years together, Sonny had just about convinced herself she was destined to be “the girlfriend forever.” While friends and family eagerly waited for the proposal, Adi seemed determined to keep everyone, especially Sonny, guessing just a little longer.

Then, in June 2026, during a summer weekend in the Hamptons, Adi finally made his move.

He got down on one knee and asked Sonny to spend forever with him.

Yes.

Somehow, through the happy tears and excitement, she managed to say the easiest “yes” of her life.
