# Night Glass reference screens

These are the starter's own screens, frozen at the version that passed the design lint.
They don't change when your app does: `npm run visual:sheet` lays them next to your
current screens so you can compare the look (glass, gradient, verb colors, spacing,
type) while the content becomes yours.

The screenshot baselines in `tests/visual/__screens__/` are different: they track your
app and get updated (with a reason) whenever a screen intentionally changes.
