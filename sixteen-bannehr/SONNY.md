# Sonny, this is your book

It is about our house, and it is yours to finish. It lives at the address I sent you. On
your iPhone, open it in Safari and tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**. From then on it
opens like an app, even without a connection.

## Reading

- **Turn pages** by swiping (up and down on the phone, sideways on the iPad), with the
  arrow keys, or by tapping the edge of a page.
- **Contents**, bottom left, takes you to any chapter.
- **Night**, bottom right, sets the book in dark ink for the evening.
- In a **before / after**, drag the round handle; on the phone, tap the picture.
- In a **plan**, tap a number to read what it marks.

## Editing

Tap **Edit**. The first time, it asks who is holding the pen, so your notes and hearts are
signed in your colour.

- **Words:** tap any title, caption or paragraph and type. Select words for **B**, *I*, a
  link, or a pull quote. Tap anywhere else when you are done.
- **Photos:** tap an empty picture to choose from your Photos, or tap **Replace** on one
  that is already there. **Focal point** lets you drag a cross to the part that must
  always show. **Alt & credit** is a line describing the photo, for screen readers.
- **Hearts:** tap ♡ on any option (on a crowded page it sits on the photo). Tick
  **Compare** on two or three to see them side by side.
- **Notes:** tap **+ Note**, then tap where it should go. Drag it to move it. **Resolve**
  tucks it away once it's answered.
- **Pages:** the strip along the bottom shows every spread. Tap one to go there, press
  and drag to reorder. **+ Add spread**, **Template** (a new layout; nothing you wrote is
  lost), **Duplicate**, **Move** and **Delete** act on the spread you are looking at.
- **Chapters:** on the Contents spread, tap a title to rename it; use the arrows to
  reorder; **+ Add a chapter** at the end.
- **Mistakes:** **↶ Undo** goes back one step at a time, as far as you like.
- **Editions:** before a big change, **Editions → Save an edition**. **Restore** brings
  it back, and Undo takes you forward again.

Everything saves itself as you go. Tap **Done** to read again.

## Sending it to me

The book lives on your phone; mine lives on mine. To share:

1. **Edit → Share → Send the book…**, then AirDrop or Messages it to me.
2. When I send you one, save it to Files, then **Edit → Share → Open a .zip…**.

Opening a book replaces the one on the phone, so let's take turns.

**Print or save as PDF** in the same place makes a printable copy, one spread per page.
